Nerd Fonts icons in the ulc Mono Nerd Font build
================================================

The Nerd Font build of ulc Mono (dist/ulc-mono-nerd, attached to a release as
ulc-fonts-nerd-<version>.zip) is ulc Mono with extra icon glyphs added by the Nerd Fonts
patcher (https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts, MIT licence for the patcher).

Parts and who owns them
- ulc Mono letters, digits, punctuation and our own icons (U+F8000 and up): ulab24,
  under the ulab24 Font Licence 1.0 (ulab24-Font-Licence-1.0.txt, shipped alongside).
- The added icons come from other projects, each under its own licence and kept by their
  authors. The patcher adds, among others: Powerline and Powerline Extra symbols, Font
  Awesome, Devicons, Seti-UI, Octicons, Codicons, Material Design Icons, Weather Icons,
  Font Logos, Pomicons and IEC Power Symbols. Their copyright notices and licence texts are
  in the Nerd Fonts repository (LICENSE and the license files under it), at the version of
  the patcher image used for the build.
- Brand and operating-system logos in those sets are trademarks of their owners. ulab24
  does not redraw them and claims no rights in them.

Rules for this build
1. Ship this notice, the ulab24 Font Licence and the Nerd Fonts licence files together
   with the patched font files.
2. The icon glyphs are not covered by the ulab24 Font Licence; use them under their own
   licences.
3. Before the first public release, copy the licence files from the exact Nerd Fonts
   version used into this folder and list them here. This notice names the sets but
   does not replace their licence texts. Not legal advice.
